1. Try Googling it
2. Check the official docs
3. Ask in Slack
4. Ask your team lead

When you create a Pull Request, a teammate will review it. They're not criticizing you — they're helping you learn. This is how professional teams work.

You have teammates, a manager, a deadline, and a real product. You're learning how actual software companies work.
